DAY 6 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 06

🏪 Welcome to Our Pretend Shop

🏪 🏷️ 🪙

A shop is a place where people may exchange money for goods or services. Our classroom shop uses drawn tokens and picture cards—not real money. Price tags tell us the cost in the pretend game. Real prices and currencies vary around the world, and adults handle actual purchases. A fair shop welcomes everyone regardless of what their family can afford.

Try together: Look at picture cards costing one, two and three pretend tokens. Match each to a group of large drawn tokens.

Talk together: How can we tell what something costs in our pretend game?

DAY 7 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 07

🔢 Compare and Count

🔢 🪙 🪙

We can compare two pretend prices by counting picture tokens. A picture costing two tokens needs fewer tokens than one costing four. Counting helps us make choices, but buying more is not always better. We can choose an affordable, useful item or decide not to shop. Practice is free and no real financial information belongs in this lesson.

Try together: Draw or point to two tokens and four tokens; select which picture price is smaller.

Talk together: What can you do when the price is more than the tokens you have?

DAY 8 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 08

🐷 A Small Saving Goal

🐷 📚 🌱

A saving goal is something we plan for later. In a pretend story, a picture book costs five tokens and our character has three. We can count how many more pretend tokens are needed. Families may have urgent needs that come before saving, so we never shame anyone who cannot put money aside. Children do not need a bank account.

Try together: Put three large paper-token pictures in a drawn jar and count two more pictures toward five.

Talk together: Why is it okay for a saving goal to take time?

DAY 9 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 09

🤲 Sharing and Community Choices

🤲 💛 📖

People may choose to share time, supplies or resources, but sharing must be voluntary and safe. A kind act such as reading with someone can cost no money. We ask caregivers before giving away belongings, and no one has to donate to participate. There are many ways to contribute without spending.

Try together: Sort picture cards into free caring actions and pretend store purchases. Choose one kind action.

Talk together: How can you be helpful even without money?

DAY 10 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 10

🛒 My Thoughtful Shopping Plan

🛒 📚 🧸

A plan can help our pretend shopper choose carefully. Suppose we have five pretend tokens: a book picture costs three and a toy picture costs four. We cannot choose both using only five. We compare, decide and explain the trade-off. Choosing to wait is also a thoughtful option. Family financial details stay private.

Try together: Use five drawn tokens to select one pretend item or decide to save, then explain the choice to a caregiver.

Talk together: What did you choose, and what did you wait for?
