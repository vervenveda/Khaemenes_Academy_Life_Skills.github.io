DAY 11 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 11

📝 My Picture Budget

📝 🪙 🏠

A budget is a plan for choosing how to use limited resources. Our pretend character has six picture tokens and can plan for something needed, something enjoyable and something saved. In a family, adults handle actual bills and budgets. Children are learning choices, not responsibility for household expenses.

Try together: Draw six large tokens and place them beside picture cards for a need, a want and future saving.

Talk together: How did you decide what came first?

DAY 12 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 12

🐷 My Saving Jar Plan

🐷 📚 🟡

A saving goal tells us what we are preparing for in the future. Our imaginary learner wants a book costing five pretend tokens and currently has two. By adding three pretend tokens, the goal is reached. Families may have circumstances that make saving difficult; nobody should feel ashamed or pressured to save real money.

Try together: Draw two tokens in a jar and add three more picture tokens. Count the total.

Talk together: Why might someone need to change a saving goal?

DAY 13 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 13

⚖️ Compare Two Choices

⚖️ 🛒 🧸

Sometimes we cannot choose everything at once. With six pretend tokens, a picture item costing four leaves two, while an item costing six leaves zero. Either choice may make sense in a story. Children can explain their thinking and adults can help when something is confusing. We never ask learners to reveal family finances.

Try together: Compare a pretend purchase costing four tokens with one costing six. Draw how many tokens remain.

Talk together: How could waiting be a good choice?

DAY 14 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 14

🔄 Improve My Money Plan

🔄 ☂️ 💭

Plans can change when new needs arise. Our pretend shopper planned for a toy but learns they need an umbrella for rainy weather. We can revise the picture plan and explain why. This is a pretend example, not advice to children to manage real emergencies or family needs. A grown-up can help us make another safe choice.

Try together: Change one picture card on your pretend budget and explain the new order.

Talk together: What new information changed your decision?

DAY 15 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 15

🌟 My First Money Plan Showcase

🌟 🐷 📘

Today we share a pretend plan with a trusted grown-up. We may point to pictures, explain aloud or show a drawing. Tell how many tokens there are, which picture comes first, what you might save for and what choice you changed. A caregiver observes thinking and counting. A quiz score does not certify financial competence or replace an adult's decisions.

Try together: Present your budget picture, show one saving goal and explain one trade-off to a caregiver.

Talk together: What are you proud to have learned about thoughtful choices?
