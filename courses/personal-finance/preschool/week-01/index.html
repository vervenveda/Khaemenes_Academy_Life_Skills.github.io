DAY 1 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 01

🪙 Money Around the World

🪙 🌍 💵

Families in different places use different currencies. Coins and paper notes may look different and have different values. Money is a tool people use to pay for goods and services, but many helpful things such as kindness and friendship do not require money. Children only explore pretend coins or large pictures, never real coins that could be choking hazards.

Try together: Look at two large drawings of different coins or notes, and describe shapes or symbols.

Talk together: Can money look different in different places?

DAY 2 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 02

🏠 Needs and Wants

🏠 💧 🧸

Needs are things important for health and safety, such as suitable food, water and shelter. Wants are things we may enjoy, such as an extra toy or special decoration. Families face different costs and circumstances; no learner should be embarrassed about what their family can buy. Grown-ups make real household decisions.

Try together: Sort pictures of water, shelter, a toy and food into needs and wants, discussing how situations can differ.

Talk together: Why do we think about needs before optional purchases?

DAY 3 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 03

🔢 Count Pretend Tokens

🔢 🟡 🟡

Counting helps us plan. One, two, three, four, five: each pretend token is one object in our counting game, not a real currency value. We count each picture once and can check by pointing again. When objects are difficult to see or handle, we count aloud or use large raised picture cards.

Try together: Count five large paper token drawings, then make groups of two and three.

Talk together: How can you check that you counted every token once?

DAY 4 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 04

🐷 Save for Later

🐷 🫙 🌱

Saving means choosing to keep something for a future purpose instead of using it immediately. Families save in different ways and may have limited money available. We can practice saving with pretend tokens, drawings, or time spent planning, without asking children to contribute real money. A trusted adult makes choices about accounts and finances.

Try together: Move three pretend picture tokens into a drawn saving jar and tell a story about a future goal.

Talk together: What is one thing you might plan for later?

DAY 5 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 05

🛍️ Thoughtful Choices

🛍️ 📚 💛

When we choose one thing, we may have to wait for another. That is called a trade-off. We can think, compare, ask questions and make pretend choices kindly. Some things cost money, while sharing a story, playing a word game or helping someone may be free. No child is responsible for solving a household's money problems.

Try together: In a pretend store with five picture tokens, choose a book picture costing three tokens or a toy picture costing five; describe your choice.

Talk together: What could you do when you cannot choose both today?
