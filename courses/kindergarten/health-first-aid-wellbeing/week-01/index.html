Day 1: Handwashing Steps

Practice five handwashing steps with picture cards.

Wet hands, use soap, scrub for about 20 seconds, rinse and dry. Wash before handling food and after using the bathroom.

My evidence: Draw and number five steps.

Identify safe food decisions.

Food traditions differ. A caregiver must check allergies and food safety. Never taste unknown plants, medicines or drinks.

Day 4: Movement for Everyone

Choose accessible healthy movement.

With adult approval movement may include rolling, walking, gentle stretching, dancing or assisted play. Respect each person's body.
