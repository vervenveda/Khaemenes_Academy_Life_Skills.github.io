Kindergarten · Life Skills Learning Garden

A new step beyond Preschool

Explore 16 Kindergarten learning pathways through planning, counting, describing choices, inclusive cooperation and caregiver-supervised practice. Every activity can use paper, pictures, words or accessible communication; no purchase or online account is required.

Current release: This is the Kindergarten pathway and skills map. The links below intentionally open existing Preschool foundational practice as optional review. They are not Kindergarten exams, and their saved progress is not a Kindergarten grade or credential. Dedicated Kindergarten daily lessons and mastery checks will be developed independently.

Progression standards

Kindergarten learning will build on familiar Preschool themes while increasing sequencing, vocabulary, decision-making and portfolio evidence. Formal weekly progression, quizzes and caregiver assessment will use independent Kindergarten records once the dedicated classrooms are ready.
