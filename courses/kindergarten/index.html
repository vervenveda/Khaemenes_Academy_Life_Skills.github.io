A new step beyond Preschool

Explore 16 Kindergarten learning pathways through planning, counting, describing choices, inclusive cooperation and caregiver-supervised practice. Every activity can use paper, pictures, words or accessible communication; no purchase or online account is required.

Current release: Kindergarten now contains 8 dedicated pathways and 8 foundation-review pathways. Culinary, Home Management, Gardening, Creativity, Health, Personal Finance, Sustainability and Emergency Readiness have independent Kindergarten lessons, records and mastery checks. The remaining eight links intentionally open Preschool foundational practice as optional review; Preschool progress never becomes a Kindergarten grade or credential.

Progression standards

Dedicated Kindergarten classrooms use independent Kindergarten learner records, weekly mastery gates and caregiver review. Foundation-review pathways remain clearly separated from Kindergarten grading until their dedicated classrooms are built. The established stage-first Kindergarten URLs are formally documented in the Life Skills route manifest and will not be moved simply to match the Preschool folder pattern.
