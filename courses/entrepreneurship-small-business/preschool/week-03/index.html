DAY 11 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 11

💡 Choose My Big Idea

💡 🖼️ 💛

A project begins with a small, useful goal. We can choose an imaginary product or service that helps our classroom or family. It may be a bookmark, picture organizer or pretend welcome sign. A caregiver reviews the idea before practice; no family should feel pressure to spend money.

Try together: Select one of three safe picture ideas and explain its purpose.

Talk together: Whose need would your idea support?

DAY 12 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 12

💡 Make a Simple Picture Plan

💡 🖼️ 💛

A simple plan shows a goal, safe materials and the order of steps. We can arrange pictures of drawing, checking and sharing a pretend creation. Real-world business permits and purchases are adult matters, not preschool requirements.

Try together: Arrange three cards: plan, create on paper, and show caregiver.

Talk together: What should happen before creating the project?

DAY 13 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 13

💡 Try the Idea Safely

💡 🖼️ 💛

We learn by trying a small safe model. A paper bookmark can be tested by placing its picture beside a pretend book. We observe whether the design is understandable. Imagined results should be called pretend rather than reported as real sales or customers.

Try together: Test a paper sign or bookmark with a caregiver and describe what happened.

Talk together: What did you observe rather than guess?

DAY 14 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 14

💡 Improve With Feedback

💡 🖼️ 💛

Feedback helps us see how an idea works for different people. Maybe the picture needs larger shapes, or the instructions should use another language. Changing a design after a kind suggestion is a success in learning, not failure.

Try together: Ask a caregiver for one suggestion and redraw a clearer version.

Talk together: What did you change to make your idea more helpful?

DAY 15 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 15

💡 My Little Idea Showcase

💡 🖼️ 💛

Present the picture of a need, your safe idea, a practice result and one improvement. Children can explain with speech, gestures, drawing or communication support. Caregivers record observed participation separately from the knowledge quiz. This is an educational project, not a real business launch.

Try together: Share a three-picture presentation and record caregiver feedback.

Talk together: How can a helpful idea respect others and stay safe?
