Our Little Safety Helpers

Learn safe choices, trusted helpers and calm communication with picture practice.

Use picture-only safety simulations. Never create hazards, set off alarms, call emergency services for practice, or require children to navigate real emergencies alone.

🛡️ My Trusted Helper Circle

🏠 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 💛

🛡️ Our Family Safety Picture

🏠 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 💛

Try together: Arrange caregiver, safe place and stay-together pictures.

🛡️ My Calm Help Words

🏠 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 💛

Use speaking, gestures or pictures to ask a grown-up for help. Never make practice emergency calls.

Try together: Practice 'I need help' in a calm imaginary story.

Talk together: What can you do if speaking is difficult?

🛡️ Everyone Belongs in the Plan

🏠 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 💛

Caregiver plans include mobility, language, sensory needs, household animals and medications handled by adults.

Try together: Add an accessibility or pet symbol to a family picture plan.

Talk together: Who is responsible for making plans accessible?
