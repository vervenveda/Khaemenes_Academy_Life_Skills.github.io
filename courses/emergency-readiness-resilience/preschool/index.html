Family guidance

Never create real hazards, activate an alarm or call emergency services for practice. Adult caregivers determine real evacuation and shelter plans using applicable local guidance. No child is asked to rescue others or manage an emergency alone. Every activity can use pictures, gestures or spoken alternatives.
