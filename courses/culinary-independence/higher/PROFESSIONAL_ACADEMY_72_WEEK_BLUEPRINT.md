# Khaemenes Culinary Academy — Professional Culinary & Restaurant Ownership Pathway

**Status:** Approved program blueprint; **not** a claim that all 72 weeks have been written or assessed. Existing CULA 101 Weeks 01–03 remain the current developed foundation. Future weeks must be implemented and independently certified before marked open.

## Access and credential integrity

Free tuition and freely accessible instructional materials. Optional real ingredients, transportation, proctored exams, professional equipment, regulatory permits and outside certifications may involve costs; provide equivalent no-cost paper/simulation learning activities wherever possible. Academy completion records are not accredited college credit, professional licenses, food-handler credentials or guarantees of employment. Hands-on proficiency requires observed demonstrations with trained evaluators and appropriate facilities. State and national rules vary: students must verify current local laws with official agencies, including health department, food establishment, business registration, zoning, fire, employment, accessibility and tax requirements.

## Structure

- 2 years × 36 weeks = **72 weeks**, each with five instructional days = **360 days**.
- 18 four-week units; each unit includes reading, accessible diagrams, guided calculations, independent case study, practical demonstration plan, weekly formative quizzes and a unit performance assessment.
- Week 18 and 54: integrative midyear assessments; Week 36: Year 1 foundation final; Week 72: Year 2 final and restaurant ownership defense. Additional unit checkpoints occur every fourth week.
- Academic knowledge checks may use the existing 80% threshold; 80% quiz completion **does not** establish professional competence. Separate evaluator rubric required for sanitation, safe knife handling, temperature monitoring, production, teamwork and service.
- All learners can use the local dietary companion to select vegan, vegetarian, pescatarian, no pork, dairy-free, allergen-related and other rules. Do not imply the companion certifies allergen-free, halal, kosher or medically suitable meals.
- Keep learner-specific saved work, backups, printable/exportable portfolios, educator feedback, accessible 255px layout and offline lessons; never replace established assessment records.

## Five-day weekly teaching contract

1. **Day 1 — Theory:** clear learning outcomes, vocabulary, fully worked examples, accessible diagrams, primary sources and relevant law with jurisdiction and verification date.
2. **Day 2 — Instructor demonstration:** safety checks, equipment alternatives, measured quantities, process images and a video/text equivalent where available.
3. **Day 3 — Guided laboratory:** adult-supervised practice or complete paper simulation, observation worksheet, control points and dietary adaptations.
4. **Day 4 — Independent application:** scenario, scaled recipe, costing or service problem, research citations and reflection.
5. **Day 5 — Review:** formative quiz, documented feedback, performance rubric and remediation/retry pathway.

## Legal and safety curriculum

Cover foodborne hazards, time/temperature control, contamination and cross-contact, cleaning and sanitizing, employee health, allergens and labeling, receiving/storage, HACCP principles, recall and traceability, food establishment permits, inspections, commercial kitchens, fire/building/zoning rules, consumer protection, business entity choice, insurance, contracts and leases, payroll, wage/hour and anti-discrimination requirements, taxes, accessibility and food advertising claims. Distinguish federal, state, county, municipal and international jurisdictions. Never substitute a generic legal checklist for local regulatory approval.

## Career and restaurant ownership capstone

Every student produces a documented restaurant concept and target customer analysis; inclusive menus with ingredient and allergen controls; standardized recipes and costing; supplier and waste plans; staffing and labor model; kitchen flow and safety management; pricing, sales forecasts, break-even, cash flow and sensitivity models; marketing and service plans; permits/insurance research; emergency continuity; and a written/oral defense with revisions. Final portfolio must distinguish verified practical demonstrations from simulations.

## Release-quality gate for each four-week unit

- Five substantial instructional days per week; original examples, explicit calculations, accurate source attribution and meaningful practice.
- All illustrations and internal links verified, no dead buttons, offline reading and 255px responsive layout.
- Dietary adaptation and allergen safety checks tested without silently rewriting the curriculum.
- Learner-isolation, assessment gating, backup/import/export and print regression tested in Chromium.
- Reviewer rubric, remediation, accessibility and academic honesty verified.
- GitHub Actions and Pages deployment confirmed before marking a unit certified.
