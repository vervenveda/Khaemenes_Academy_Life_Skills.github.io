Welcome to Your Grade 6 Kitchen Adventure!

We build on Grade 5 with nutrition-label interpretation, decimal budgets, proportional recipe scaling, food systems, and evidence-based design revisions.

Practice first. Adult supervision always. No knives, heat or powered equipment without a qualified adult handling them.

🌉 Your Grade 5-to-Grade 6 Bridge

Review the Grade 5 Culinary Showcase and bring forward a recipe card, measurement drawing, and caregiver observations. This is an orientation, not an automatic transfer of mastery.

🌟 Review Grade 5 Week 03 🍓 Grade 5 Culinary Campus

An adult or educator should review the learner's readiness, including the Grade 5 project, measurements and practical safety evidence. Grade 6 preview learning is available to every learner, whether or not an earlier digital portfolio exists.
