# Khaemenes Academy · Culinary Independence
## Three-Week Course Blueprint · v1.0

**Pathway:** culinary-independence  
**Program:** Life Skills · Preschool through Higher Learning  
**Duration:** 3 weeks / 15 instructional days · **Status: curriculum scaffold, not completed lessons**  
**Predecessor:** [36-week archival planning outline](./OUTLINE.md) (preserved; **not** the current delivery schedule).

### Course outcome and capstone
Design, budget, prepare safely, and document a balanced meal appropriate to learner ability.

### Culinary ingredient and dietary policy
All Academy-authored Culinary menus, recipe examples, game food cards, shopping lists and printable meals must be **pork-free**. Do not include bacon, ham, pork sausage, pork ribs, lard, pancetta, prosciutto or pork-derived ingredients. Where a comparable dish is desired, use **turkey alternatives** (for example turkey bacon, turkey ham or turkey sausage) with accurate product labeling and preparation instructions; vegetarian alternatives are welcome. **Check ingredient labels:** processed turkey products and cheese may have other dietary or allergen considerations, and recipes must identify age-related choking risks and require appropriate caregiver preparation. Do not automatically substitute turkey in an instructional explanation about allergens or ingredient labels where accurate terminology is needed. Review external recipe sources before assigning.

### Essential foundations
1. Kitchen orientation, hygiene, allergies, and safe adult supervision.
2. Ingredients, measuring, units, substitutions, and recipe literacy.
3. Equipment, preparation techniques, heat hazards, and cleanup.
4. Nutrition, dietary needs, food labels, and balanced meal planning.
5. Shopping, storage, spoilage, food budgets, and waste reduction.
6. Preparing a complete meal, timing, serving, and evaluating results.

### Week 1 — Discover and understand (Days 1–5)
Original age-adapted reading, foundational vocabulary, safety/hazard literacy, guided observation, sensory-accessible instruction and caregiver-supported practice. End with a formal **80% knowledge gate**; incomplete tasks or games do not count as a pass.

### Week 2 — Practice and apply (Days 6–10)
Step-by-step interactive sandbox, hands-on practice or safe simulation, error correction, family collaboration and accessible evidence. End with a separate reviewed **80% knowledge gate** and demonstrated safety competency where applicable.

### Week 3 — Create and demonstrate (Days 11–15)
Plan, complete, explain and refine an authentic capstone. Final knowledge assessment **≥80%**, independent practical approval, complete evidence record and parent/educator portfolio signoff before course completion.

### Per-lesson A+++ production requirements
Every daily lesson must ultimately contain: **clear measurable objectives; original in-depth reading** adapted to stage; vocabulary; worked examples and misconceptions; explicit safety or ethical boundaries; **one lightweight illustrative final-result graphic** with descriptive text; four or more progressive interactive popup steps; an age-appropriate practice game with feedback; optional educator-vetted captioned video and **complete offline text equivalent**; real-world activity or equivalent safe simulation; teacher/parent discussion prompts; printable assignment with Khaemenes Academy heading, name, date, stage, unit, and lesson; per-day activity evidence log, practical review, and remediation. Optional video or games never unlock formal gates by themselves.

### Verified progress and family evidence
- A learner-specific profile is required to save graded results; anonymous preview practice remains clearly labeled. Device-only codes are provisional, not identity verification.
- Reviewed randomized banks with answer-order shuffling and immutable scored attempt snapshots; target at least triple exam item count; a correct-answer audit and distractor review is mandatory before launch.
- At least **80% independently verified knowledge mastery** at each weekly gate and at the final; practical safety review is separately required. If a skill cannot be performed safely, preserve a simulation record while physical competency remains pending.
- Save dated activity logs and adult observations separately per day. Support private export/import, printable packets, learner separation, reattempts after feedback, narrow-screen 255px layout and keyboard access.
- A badge or confetti celebrates **practice**, but never changes mastery scores or issues credentials.
- Certificates are only eligible after three weekly knowledge passes, final examination, capstone, practical signoff and portfolio review; **no certificate engine exists yet**.

### Reuse and seasonal localization
- Search existing Academy, ARSHIF, games and Higher Learning resources before authoring duplicates; keep links to their canonical source and audit each resource before declaring it assigned.
- Family-paced, year-round or summer delivery uses the **same 15 lesson requirements**. Assign gardening to the local growing season where feasible and adapt arts projects to the learner’s chosen celebrations. Provide inclusive and indoor alternatives.
- Offline-first vanilla HTML/CSS/JS is the default learner-portal delivery. Images must have descriptive alternatives and should not require downloads to complete lessons.
