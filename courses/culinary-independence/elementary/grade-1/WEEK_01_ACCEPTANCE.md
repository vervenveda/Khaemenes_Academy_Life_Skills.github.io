# Grade 1 Culinary — Week 01 acceptance checklist

Status: source implemented, browser verification pending.

1. Open learner A, complete one practice, enter a note, reload, reopen learner A. Both values must remain.
2. Open learner B; no learner A notes, practices, scores, or caregiver data may appear.
3. A score below 80% must not award the Mastery Garden trophy. Four correct of five earns knowledge mastery, but not the trophy until all five practices and caregiver review are complete.
4. Save a JSON export. Restore it for the same learner and week: first click must preview, second click asks for two confirmations. After restore, lesson evidence remains but quiz attempts are empty.
5. Wrong learner, wrong course, invalid JSON, and cancelled confirmation must leave existing local evidence unchanged.
6. Print at desktop width and 255px viewport: Khaemenes Academy, grade, unit, week, learner and date must be readable. Confirm notes appear in the printout.
7. Check keyboard navigation and all buttons. Games on the Grade 1 landing page remain practice-only and never award formal quiz mastery.
8. Confirm no raw food preparation, heat, sharp tools or powered equipment is assigned to unsupervised children.

A source check or successful Pages deployment is not sufficient to mark these scenarios as passed.
