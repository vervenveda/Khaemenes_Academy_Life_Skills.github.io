Welcome to Your Grade 1 Kitchen Adventure!

We bring our Preschool Kitchen Helper skills into bigger questions, simple measuring, patterns and family projects.

Practice first. Adult supervision always. No knives, heat or powered equipment without a qualified adult handling them.

🌉 Your Preschool-to-Grade-1 Bridge

Families can review the three preschool Culinary weeks and carry forward dated drawings, quiz results and practical observations. This bridge is an orientation—not automatic transfer of records or proof of completion.

🌟 Review Preschool Week 03 🍓 Preschool Culinary Campus

An adult or educator should review the learner's readiness, including all three weekly knowledge checks and practical safety evidence. Grade 1 preview learning is available to every learner, whether or not a preschool record exists.

Our Grade 1 Learning Map

🍎 Week 1 · Discover and Count

Identify safe tools, count foods, compare colors, read simple picture instructions, and keep clean hands.

🍎 Open Week 1 classroom →

🥣 Week 2 · Measure and Explore

Use dry measuring play, notice full and empty containers, compare quantities, and draw observations.

🥣 Open Week 2 classroom →

🌟 Week 3 · Plan and Present

Design a safe family snack or pretend menu, collect picture evidence, practice teamwork and share a final presentation.

🌟 Open Week 3 Culinary Showcase →

All three Grade 1 weeks now include five guided learning days each, 80% mastery quizzes, saved progress, and printable evidence. Weeks 2 and 3 require mastery of the preceding week. The Culinary Showcase is the final introductory project.

🎲 Mini Kitchen Discovery Games

These are practice-only games. They never issue an 80% grade.

🎨 Food Color Match

Pick the color of a red apple.

🥄 Measuring & Counting

Count the three spoons: 🥄 🥄 🥄. How many?

🖼️ First Grade Family Project Preview

1. Ask an adult to help choose a safe pretend snack. 2. Wash hands. 3. Draw three ingredients and label their colors. 4. Count the pretend servings. 5. Explain your plan to the grown-up.

Printable evidence: Draw your snack, record the date and write or dictate one safety rule.
