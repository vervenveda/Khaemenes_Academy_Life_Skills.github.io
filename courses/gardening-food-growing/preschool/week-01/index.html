DAY 1 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 01

🌱 Meet a Seed

🌱 🌈 🌰

A seed is a tiny beginning for many plants. Some seeds are big and some are small. A seed may grow into a plant when conditions are right. We do not eat unknown seeds. Some seeds can be unsafe for young children, so our grown-up selects large, suitable items or picture cards.

Family activity: Study two pictures of seeds. Name one difference in their colors, shapes or sizes. Draw your favorite seed.

DAY 2 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 02

☀️ What Plants Need

☀️ 🌈 💧

Plants need light, water, air and room to grow. Different plants need different amounts. Too little or too much water may harm them. A grown-up helps us choose a safe place and the correct care routine.

Family activity: Match plant pictures with sunlight, water, air and growing space. Tell an adult why each matters.

DAY 3 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 03

🪴 Soil and Growing Places

🪴 🌈 🏡

Plants can grow in garden beds or suitable containers. Many plants use soil or another growing medium to support roots. Outdoor soil may contain sharp objects or animal waste; never put soil in your mouth. Wash hands after handling growing materials.

Family activity: With a grown-up, compare pictures of a pot and garden bed. Use dry paper circles as pretend soil or handle safe potting material under supervision.

DAY 4 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 04

🔢 Count Seeds and Make a Plan

🔢 🌈 📝

We can count pretend seeds and plan where a container might belong. A picture plan shows the pot, a safe place for light and an adult who will help with water. Counting helps us follow a simple plan while protecting real seeds from being swallowed.

Family activity: Count five large paper seed pictures, arrange them on a drawn pot, and ask an adult to check the plan.

DAY 5 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 05

💚 Our Mini Garden Promise

💚 🌈 🌼

Growing a plant takes patience. We can notice small changes and keep a simple picture journal. Some seeds do not sprout, and that is part of learning. A grown-up checks plant and soil safety, watering, allergies and local weather. We can practice indoors with pictures if real gardening is not possible.

Family activity: Create a family garden plan or pretend garden. Draw how you will observe it next week and name one safety rule.
