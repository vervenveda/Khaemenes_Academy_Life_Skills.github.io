Every week will have five lessons, an illustrated practice activity, a local knowledge check and caregiver observation. Lessons are seasonal and can be completed indoors with safe simulations.

Safety and accessibility

Unknown seeds, soil, plant leaves, fertilizers, pesticides, sharp equipment and tools are not children's independent activities. The grown-up decides safe materials and checks potential allergies. If outdoor gardening is inaccessible or the climate is unsuitable, picture-based and paper-container activities are equivalent learning practice.
