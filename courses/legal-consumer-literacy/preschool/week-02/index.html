DAY 6 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 06

⚖️ Needs Versus Ads

⚖️ 🖼️ 💛

Advertisements are designed to attract attention and may make a toy look irresistible. A need is not the same as an advertised want. No family has to buy anything for this class.

Try together: Identify the pretend buy-button and choose to ask a caregiver.

Talk together: What would be a respectful and safe next step?

DAY 7 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 07

⚖️ Safe Shopping Pictures

⚖️ 🖼️ 💛

Shopping can involve comparing price, usefulness and safety. Adults handle real money and purchases. Children can count drawn tokens and practice making thoughtful choices.

Try together: Compare a pretend label with two dots and another with four.

Talk together: What would be a respectful and safe next step?

DAY 8 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 08

⚖️ Keep Personal Information Private

⚖️ 🖼️ 💛

Addresses, passwords and payment numbers are private. Children should never type those details into practice forms. Paper examples use only invented information.

Try together: Cross out a pretend request for a password.

Talk together: What would be a respectful and safe next step?

DAY 9 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 09

⚖️ Receipt and Record Pictures

⚖️ 🖼️ 💛

A receipt records a purchase and may help adults check what was bought. Children can recognize a pretend item and price, without reading real household spending records.

Try together: Match a pretend receipt picture to a drawn item.

Talk together: What would be a respectful and safe next step?

DAY 10 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 10

⚖️ Speak Up About a Problem

⚖️ 🖼️ 💛

When something seems wrong or unfair, a child can explain it calmly to a trusted adult. Adults help decide next steps under local rules. No child must contact a company or agency.

Try together: Role-play saying 'I need help with this' to a caregiver.

Talk together: What would be a respectful and safe next step?
