DAY 11 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 11

⚖️ Choose a Fair Solution

⚖️ 🖼️ 💛

Fair choices consider needs, others' rights and safety. A pretend problem might involve two children who want the same book. Adults can guide a solution without forcing sharing.

Try together: Choose a fair turn-taking picture and explain why.

Talk together: What would be a respectful and safe next step?

DAY 12 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 12

⚖️ Build a Safe Choice Plan

⚖️ 🖼️ 💛

A simple choice plan includes the problem, safe options and a trusted adult. Do not include real account numbers, addresses or legal documents.

Try together: Arrange picture cards for notice, ask and decide with adult support.

Talk together: What would be a respectful and safe next step?

DAY 13 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 13

⚖️ Practice an Adult-Help Request

⚖️ 🖼️ 💛

Children may ask for help when an advertisement, request or rule seems confusing. A simple help phrase or gesture is enough. They should not manage legal problems alone.

Try together: Practice asking a caregiver to explain an unfamiliar pretend form.

Talk together: What would be a respectful and safe next step?

DAY 14 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 14

⚖️ Improve My Plan

⚖️ 🖼️ 💛

Feedback helps improve a fairness plan. A sign may need larger pictures or another language. Changes should preserve personal boundaries and access for different learners.

Try together: Redraw your plan to be clearer and more inclusive.

Talk together: What would be a respectful and safe next step?

DAY 15 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 15

⚖️ My Fair Choices Showcase

⚖️ 🖼️ 💛

Present a fictional fair-choice story using pictures, words or gestures. Explain a need, a respectful action and when an adult helps. A knowledge score alone does not certify practical or legal competence.

Try together: Share the picture story privately and record caregiver observations.

Talk together: What would be a respectful and safe next step?
