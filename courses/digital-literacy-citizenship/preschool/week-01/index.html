DAY 1 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 01

💻 Screens Are Tools

💻 🌈 💛

Devices can help us learn, create and communicate. In our digital literacy & citizenship class, we can learn with a paper picture, a story, a spoken description or a safe pretend activity. Families have different abilities, languages and resources, so no purchase is needed. Ask a trusted grown-up for guidance, and never share personal information in a public project.

Try together: Point to learning and play screen uses.

Talk together: What did you notice, and what would you try next?
