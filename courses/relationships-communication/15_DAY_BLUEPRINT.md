# Khaemenes Academy · Relationships & Communication
## Three-Week / 15-Day Course Blueprint v1.0

**Status:** foundational teaching map; full curriculum, videos, games and assessments remain in development.

### Essential subject strands
1. Self-awareness, dignity, respectful listening, and empathy.
2. Boundaries, consent, personal safety, and trusted adults.
3. Clear speaking, writing, collaboration, and accessible communication.
4. Healthy disagreement, de-escalation, and conflict repair.
5. Support networks, community relationships, online interactions, and referral.
6. Role-play and reflect on safe communication and decision-making scenarios.

**Authentic capstone:** Demonstrate respectful communication through approved, private role-play evidence.

### Program rhythm
**Week 1 (Days 1–5):** Foundations, safe conduct, essential reading and worked examples. Day 5 formal knowledge gate ≥80%.

**Week 2 (Days 6–10):** Interactive practice, family applications, research, tradeoffs and guided demonstrations. Day 10 formal knowledge gate ≥80%.

**Week 3 (Days 11–15):** Project planning, implementation, reflection, and portfolio; independently scored final knowledge gate ≥80%, plus separate parent/teacher verification of practical safety.

### Every day's full teaching bundle
An A+++ finished lesson requires age-specific original deep reading and vocabulary; an illustrated example of the finished result with alt description; interactive four-step popup with Next/Back and accessible text; playful formative practice and feedback; an age-safe practical or simulated family task; optional vetted captioned video with original offline-equivalent reading; printable Khaemenes Academy evidence (student, date, grade, course, unit, lesson), daily activity log, adult observations and corrections. An article link or game without taught concepts is not a lesson.

### Stage-specific adaptation
| Stage | Activity | Demonstration and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Preschool | Caregiver-read illustrated story, sorting/matching, simple supervised role-play | Child explanation, drawing, dated caregiver record |
| Elementary | Guided stepwise practice, realistic number/reading tasks, picture sequence | Hands-on work and a brief written or pictorial reflection |
| Middle | Research, numeracy, budgets, options and multi-step plans | Dated work, explanation and adult review |
| High | Applied critical reasoning, competing constraints, safety and ethics | Evidence portfolio, scenario solutions and practical signoff |
| Higher Learning | Research-grade analysis, advanced case studies and relevant local regulations | A documented applied project with defensible evidence |

### Mastery and safe evidence integrity
Knowledge requires formal independently checked ≥80% at each of the three gates. Practice grades, page visits, stickers and parent presence do not satisfy the exam. Recorded assessments need reviewed large question banks, randomized choice order, immutable scored snapshots, learner-separated storage and secure handling of exports. Practical safety competency requires separate supervised approval; an unsupervised simulation can teach but not certify physical performance. Parent/teacher signoff and complete capstone are additional prerequisites to an eventual certificate (not yet implemented).
