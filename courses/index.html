Life Skills Course Outlines

Sixteen practical learning pathways share a three-week, 15-day implementation standard. Preschool now has dedicated classroom structures across all 16 pathways; Kindergarten has 8 dedicated courses and 8 foundation-review routes; Culinary is the current vertical model into later Academy stages.

Release status varies by stage. Blueprints remain planning references, while completed stage classrooms use independent learner records, mastery gates, caregiver/teacher review and stage-specific validation. See the route manifest and completion trackers before interpreting a pathway as complete at every stage.
