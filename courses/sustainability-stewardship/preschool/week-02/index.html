DAY 6 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 06

💧 The Water-Saving Picture Game

💧 🚰 🖼️

Water supports living things. Keeping taps closed when water is not needed can help conserve it, but only an adult should supervise real taps and drinking-water decisions. Some communities lack reliable water; this lesson does not require using or wasting a drop. We can compare drawings and discuss caring choices.

Try together: Sort two drawings: a tap left running without a reason and a tap turned off after use. Choose the careful action.

Talk together: How can we learn to save water without turning on a tap?

DAY 7 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 07

💡 Energy Helpers

💡 📖 🏠

Lights and appliances use energy. We can notice when a light is on in an empty room and ask an adult whether it can be switched off. Some lights are needed for safety, accessibility or care, so grown-ups decide. Children never handle plugs, electrical panels or damaged cords.

Try together: Compare a picture of an unused lamp with a lamp lighting someone reading. Ask an adult which may safely be switched off.

Talk together: When is leaving a light on helpful?

DAY 8 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 08

🧩 Reuse or Ask an Adult?

♻️ 📄 🧩

Clean paper, a sound book or a reusable cloth bag might be used again. Broken glass, batteries, unknown containers and dirty or sharp objects need adult handling. Recycling rules vary by community, and sometimes a material cannot be recycled locally. We can sort *pictures* without touching waste.

Try together: Sort six large picture cards into safe reuse, ask an adult, or check local rules.

Talk together: Why is asking an adult a good choice for an unknown item?

DAY 9 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 09

🧸 Repair With Care

🧸 📚 🔧

Looking after useful things often saves materials. A ripped picture page might be taped by a grown-up; a loose button or electrical problem should never be repaired by a preschooler. Children can tell an adult, draw what happened and help organize safe belongings. A pretend paper repair is a complete alternative.

Try together: Put three pictures in order: notice the damage, ask a grown-up, and show a safe repair or replacement plan.

Talk together: How can you help when something breaks without handling tools?

DAY 10 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 10

🌎 Our Family Conservation Challenge

🌍 💚 🌱

A conservation challenge is a small plan to use resources thoughtfully. Every family faces different budgets, housing arrangements, climates and accessibility needs. A child may choose to use both sides of a clean drawing sheet, care for a book, or remind an adult about a safe water habit. The goal is to notice a practical action—not to make children responsible for household costs.

Try together: Choose one no-cost, safe conservation action on a picture chart, add a date, and tell a grown-up what you noticed.

Talk together: Which choice fits your home and family's needs?
