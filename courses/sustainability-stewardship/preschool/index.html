Family guidance

Use safe, clean paper pictures rather than real rubbish, sharp objects, batteries or unknown chemicals. Never require families to waste water or energy for an experiment. Children are not responsible for household bills or global environmental problems. Activities adapt to local seasons, accessibility and resources.
