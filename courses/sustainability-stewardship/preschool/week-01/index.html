DAY 1 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 01

🌍 Our Shared Home

🌍 🌳 🐦

People, plants and animals share environments. Air, clean water, soil and sunlight support living things in different ways. Caring for our surroundings can start with noticing what is around us. Different communities and climates have different needs; we can help without expensive supplies. We never handle unfamiliar wildlife or plants alone.

Try together: Look through a window or at a picture of nature. Name two living things and one thing they may need.

Talk together: Why do plants and animals need safe places?

DAY 2 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 02

💧 Water Is Precious

💧 🚰 🌱

Water is important for people, plants and animals. Many households do not have easy access to safe water, so our activities must never require wasting it. A trusted adult manages taps, sanitation and drinking water safety. We can practice saving water by pointing to a picture of a closed tap, not by experimenting with a real tap alone.

Try together: Choose between pictures of a running unattended tap and a closed tap; explain a safe water-saving habit.

Talk together: Who can help us keep water safe at home?

DAY 3 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 03

♻️ Reduce, Reuse, Sort

♻️ 📄 🛍️

Reducing means avoiding unnecessary waste. Reusing means using a safe item again when appropriate. Recycling rules differ by location; not everything belongs in a recycling bin. Preschoolers must never handle actual trash, sharp cans, batteries or unknown containers. Clean paper pictures are enough for our game.

Try together: Sort drawings of a clean paper sheet, reusable bag and damaged battery into adult-directed categories.

Talk together: Why should a grown-up handle batteries and unknown trash?

DAY 4 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 04

🧸 Care for Our Things

🧸 📚 💛

Taking care of a toy, book or clothing can help it last longer. We can store books gently, keep safe toys in their places and ask an adult about repairs. Broken glass, sharp plastic, electrical items and sewing needles are adult-only hazards. If we cannot repair something, a paper picture can show a safe solution.

Try together: Sequence three pictures: notice a torn book, tell a grown-up, and ask for a safe repair.

Talk together: What can you do to help belongings last?

DAY 5 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 05

🌱 Little Earth Helpers

🌱 🌍 🌈

Every family can choose small, realistic ways to care for their surroundings. We might reuse the back of clean paper, switch off an unused light with caregiver permission, or observe plants without damaging them. Children are not responsible for solving global environmental problems. We can celebrate one safe action and keep learning together.

Try together: Choose a picture-based earth-helper plan with one water, material or nature habit; explain it to your caregiver.

Talk together: What one safe habit would you like to practice?
