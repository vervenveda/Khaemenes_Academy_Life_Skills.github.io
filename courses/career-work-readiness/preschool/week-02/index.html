DAY 6 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 06

🧰 My Strengths

🧰 🌈 💛

People learn in different ways. Some like drawing, others enjoy organizing, listening, fixing pretend problems or comforting friends. A strength is something we can practice, not a label that limits our future. Everyone deserves meaningful opportunities regardless of disability, language or family income.

Try together: Choose three picture cards showing interests; describe a skill you would enjoy practicing.

Talk together: How can classmates have different strengths and still work together?

DAY 7 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 07

🧰 Practice a Routine

🧰 🌈 💛

A routine is a set of steps we do in an order. Helpers might gather safe materials, complete a task, then tidy their workspace. Preschoolers can practice arranging paper cards rather than using workplace equipment. Routines can include extra time, visual supports or breaks for different learners.

Try together: Arrange pictures of prepare, practice and tidy; then act out the steps with pretend props.

Talk together: Which step should come before putting materials away?

DAY 8 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 08

🧰 Solve a Small Problem

🧰 🌈 💛

Sometimes a pretend task does not work on the first try. We can notice what happened, ask a trusted adult for help and choose another safe approach. A missing picture card is a problem we can solve without blaming anyone. Making a mistake offers information about what to try next.

Try together: Imagine a missing puzzle piece and choose between asking for help, checking nearby and giving up.

Talk together: What could you try when a plan needs changing?

DAY 9 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 09

🧰 Speak Kindly at Work

🧰 🌈 💛

People cooperate more easily when they listen and speak respectfully. Helpers might say 'May I help?', 'Please explain' or 'Thank you'. People also communicate with gestures, pictures and devices. A child may say no to unsafe tasks and ask for an adult instead of obeying every request.

Try together: Role-play asking a teammate for help and listening to their reply.

Talk together: What can we say when instructions are confusing?

DAY 10 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 10

🧰 Rest Is Important

🧰 🌈 💛

People need rest, nourishment and safe conditions. Work is not a reason to ignore discomfort or keep going when tired. Everyone's abilities and energy are different, and accommodations help people participate. Preschoolers should never be expected to perform actual adult labor.

Try together: Choose a picture schedule with safe play, a short helping activity and a rest break.

Talk together: How can a team respect someone who needs a break?
