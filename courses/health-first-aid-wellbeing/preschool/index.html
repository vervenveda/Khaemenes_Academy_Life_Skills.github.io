Safety for families everywhere

Preschool learning is about recognizing when to seek help, not performing medical procedures. Never use real medicine, blood, sharp objects, chemicals or actual emergency phone calls in games. Emergency systems and phone numbers differ by country. Activities have paper, picture and spoken alternatives and never require a purchase.
