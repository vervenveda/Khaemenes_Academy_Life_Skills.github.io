DAY 11 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 11

💬 My Voice Matters

💬 💛 🗣️

A child may say when something hurts, feels strange or makes them uncomfortable. We can communicate by talking, pointing, signing or choosing pictures. Grown-ups should listen and help. We can ask questions and explain our needs without having to share personal health information publicly.

Try together: Choose a picture that says 'I feel okay', 'I am worried' or 'I need help'; practice showing it to a trusted adult.

Talk together: How can you tell somebody you need help?

DAY 12 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 12

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 My Trusted Helper Circle

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 🏠 💛

Families and caregiving arrangements differ. A trusted helper may be a parent, caregiver, teacher or another responsible adult selected by the family. Make a private helper picture list with your grown-up. Do not put real names, telephone numbers, medical details or home addresses in public lesson pages or shared art.

Try together: Draw two or three helper symbols and talk privately about when to ask each person for help.

Talk together: What should you do when your first trusted helper cannot answer?

DAY 13 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 13

📘 My Healthy Day Plan

🫧 🌙 📘

Healthy routines may include washing hands, eating appropriate foods, drinking water, resting and moving in comfortable ways. Every family's schedule, abilities, climate and resources are different. A simple picture plan helps us remember routines without judging other people's choices. It is okay to change a plan with caregiver support.

Try together: Arrange three large picture cards showing a healthy routine; add one comfortable rest or play choice.

Talk together: Which routine is easiest for you to remember?

DAY 14 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 14

🛟 Practice the Help Plan

🛟 🛑 🤝

When there is a concerning situation, a child can notice, move to safety if possible, and find a trusted adult. In a serious emergency, adults should contact appropriate local emergency services. This lesson is picture-only rehearsal; never stage hazards, touch real medical supplies or place practice calls to emergency numbers.

Try together: Use picture cards to describe a pretend situation, a safe place and a trusted adult to tell.

Talk together: Why should we not investigate a dangerous place ourselves?

DAY 15 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 15

🌟 My Family Wellness Showcase

🌟 📘 💛

We have learned about healthy routines, feelings, trusted helpers and asking for help. Share a private picture plan or spoken story with your caregiver: show one healthy habit, one helper and one safe response. A responsible adult documents what the child can explain. A quiz score does not establish first-aid competence or replace adult safety decisions.

Try together: Present your three-part private wellness plan, explain a safety choice and invite caregiver observations.

Talk together: What was the most useful thing you learned about asking for help?
