# Khaemenes Academy · Health, First Aid & Wellbeing
## 15-Day Life Skills Curriculum Blueprint v1.0

**Course duration:** Three weeks · 15 daily lessons · **Status:** production blueprint, not fifteen authored lessons.

### Core outcomes
1. Wellbeing foundations, hygiene, rest, nutrition, and movement.
2. Recognizing emergencies, calling for help, and consent.
3. First aid concepts, supplies, infection prevention, and training boundaries.
4. Stress, emotions, mental health support, and trusted resources.
5. Accessing care, health information literacy, and personal advocacy.
6. Create a safe personal and household wellness response plan.

**Capstone:** Document a home wellness plan; obtain separate qualified training for any certified procedures.

### Week 1 · Foundations, safety, concepts and first practice (Days 1–5)
Accessible, substantial original reading, examples, vocabulary, modeled practice and one 80% formally scored gate at week's end. Teach child-safe alternatives where required.

### Week 2 · Games, progressive competence, family applications (Days 6–10)
Guided practice, contextual exercises, formative game feedback, dated logs, family review and an independently assessed Week 2 knowledge gate ≥80%.

### Week 3 · Portfolio, capstone and competency (Days 11–15)
Complete a genuine project, correct errors, show evidence, pass the final formal knowledge gate ≥80%, and record distinct safety/practical approval. Issue no certificate from practice alone.

### Mandatory daily lesson components
1. Clearly measurable objective and stage-specific original reading with illustrations and worked examples.
2. Four or more interactive sandbox popup steps, accessible text equivalent, and a meaningful visual of the final outcome.
3. Safe, non-graded practice game, formative feedback, remediation and real-world family activity or accessible simulation.
4. Optional vetted captioned lesson video with a full original **offline text equivalent**, never a prerequisite.
5. Printable Academy-heading packet with student, date, grade/stage, unit and lesson; daily activity log and parent observation.
6. Independent randomized formal assessments with saved answer snapshots, verified 80% mastery and distinct practical competence decisions.

### Age localization (five separate releases)
- **Preschool:** short caregiver-read stories, visual matching, illustrated safe steps, drawing and observed demonstration; no unsupervised hazards.
- **Elementary:** clear guided reading, picture-rich application, supervised task, basic recording and age-appropriate numeric work.
- **Middle:** multi-step research, budgets, comparisons, self-reflection and household problem solving.
- **High School:** realistic tradeoffs, ethics, responsible independence, documented evidence and source checking.
- **Higher Learning / Adult:** advanced research, professional contexts, jurisdiction-aware rules, complex project and analytic portfolio.

### Gating, privacy, and QA
- Week 2 opens only after verified Week 1 ≥80%; Week 3 only after verified Week 2 ≥80%. Complete capstone and practical review after final ≥80% before course signoff.
- Practice stickers, game scores and checkboxes never grant formal mastery. Hands-on risk or physical skills require separate safe supervised verification.
- Preserve scored attempt snapshots, learner separation, versioned course keys, local export/import and parent/educator signoff without public student data.
- Provide 255px-responsive accessible HTML/JS, keyboard controls, offline-first teaching, readable typography and page-separated print evidence.
- Adapt holidays and seasons to locale; offer indoor and culturally inclusive alternatives. All fifteen days remain required regardless of summer scheduling.
