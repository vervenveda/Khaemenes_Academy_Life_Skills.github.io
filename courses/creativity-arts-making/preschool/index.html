Family guidance

Use large paper pieces, child-safe approved art supplies and observation instead of hazardous tools. Adults supervise all hands-on activities. No small choking-risk parts, sharp needles, hot glue, heated equipment or chemical mixing. Activities may be completed by pointing, describing or using a caregiver-made model.

Optional celebrations may be chosen by each family. The core lessons are secular and accessible year-round.
