DAY 6 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 06

🔴🟡 Pattern Parade

🔴 🟡 🔴 🟡

A pattern repeats in a way we can notice. Red, yellow, red, yellow is a repeating color pattern. Shapes and sounds can make patterns too. Artists use patterns on textiles, buildings, pictures and everyday objects. Every culture makes beautiful designs, and we can create our own instead of copying someone's special design without permission.

Try together: Point to the missing part of a circle-square-circle-square sequence, then create your own.

Talk together: What part comes next, and how do you know?

DAY 7 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 07

🧩 Design Something Helpful

📦 🖍️ 💡

Designers notice a need and imagine a way to help. A picture label can help someone find their books; a large bright symbol may help someone recognize a basket. Good design welcomes different abilities and needs. A design drawing can be useful without buying materials or building anything.

Try together: Draw or describe a large picture label for a safe toy or book bin.

Talk together: Who could your design help?

DAY 8 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 08

🌈 Color-and-Shape Challenge

🔵 🔺 🟢

Artists choose color and shape together. We can make a calm pattern with repeated blue circles or a lively one with warm-colored triangles. Colors have different meanings to different people. It is fine to describe shapes if we cannot see certain colors, or choose tactile paper with adult support.

Try together: Choose three large shapes and design two arrangements; explain which is easiest to recognize.

Talk together: How could another learner identify your shape design without relying only on color?

DAY 9 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 09

♻️ Making With Safe Materials

📄 🧻 ♻️

Many useful artworks begin with materials already at home. A clean cereal box can become a picture frame in our imagination; paper can become a collage. Adults inspect and prepare all materials. Preschoolers do not handle staples, sharp needles, hot glue, plastic bags or small choking-risk scraps. If suitable materials are unavailable, draw a pretend project.

Try together: Sort drawings of paper, a crayon and sharp scissors into adult-approved activities and adult-only tools.

Talk together: What is one material we can reuse safely with help?

DAY 10 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 10

🤝 Make Art Together

🤝 🖼️ 💜

A creative team listens and takes turns. One child may pick the shapes while another tells a story; a grown-up can help record ideas. We ask before touching someone else's work. When ideas differ, we can combine safe ideas or make two designs. Teamwork is not a contest, and asking for help is a strength.

Try together: Create a shared picture plan with an adult: decide who will choose, draw or describe each part.

Talk together: What kind words could you use when someone wants a different shape?
