# Khaemenes Academy
## Caregiving & Family Responsibilities — Course Outline v0.1

**Pathway ID:** FAM  
**Status:** Design outline only; not a complete approved course  
**Duration target:** 36 instructional weeks, six units of six weeks; stage-specific course variants to follow  
**Core sequence:** Explore → Practice → Demonstrate → Reflect

### Learning purpose
Develop safe, applicable personal independence through authentic practice, informed choices, and documented evidence.

### Capstone evidence
Develop an accessible care-support plan with explicit limits on what minors should undertake.

### Assessment and learner evidence
- Every week: visible original reading, knowledge checks, guided practical exercise, offline printable record with **Khaemenes Academy**, student name, date, stage/grade, unit and lesson.
- Formative practice does **not** count as passing a mastery gate.
- Formal knowledge assessment requires **80% verified mastery** to unlock the next formal lesson or stage; question banks, answer order and attempt snapshots will be added during implementation.
- Practical competency requires a separate safety-appropriate supervised demonstration and reviewer signoff; quiz-only results do not establish practical competence.
- Midterm after Week 18 and final after Week 36 with independent mastery gates; relevant capstone evidence and portfolio review required before a completion record.
- Students may re-practice and reassess without punishment; record attempts transparently and protect private data.

### Stage-specific adaptation requirements
| Preschool | Recognize, name and role-play with a trusted adult; never assign dangerous independent tasks. |
| Elementary | Follow short supervised procedures, picture logs and real-world vocabulary. |
| Middle | Plan, estimate, compare and complete safe multi-step work with adult review. |
| High | Execute practical projects, budgets, critiques and documented skill demonstrations. |
| Higher Learning/Adult | Apply advanced independent decisions, workplace contexts, local rules and portfolio evidence. |

### Safety, inclusion, and provenance
No unsafe unsupervised activity by minors. Use alternatives for learners without equipment, internet, transportation, money or safe access to a household task. Provide readable text, accessible instructions, optional translation, adjustable scale, print layouts and no mandatory third-party dependencies. Professional certifications and jurisdiction-specific legal/medical/financial guidance are outside the course's authority; link to verified authoritative references later.
