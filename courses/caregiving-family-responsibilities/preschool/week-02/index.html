DAY 6 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 06

🤲 Care for Shared Belongings

🤲 🖼️ 💛

Shared belongings last longer when handled carefully. Ask before borrowing and put clean toys or books in adult-approved places. No child must clean unsafe waste.

Try together: Practice carefully returning a book to its shelf.

Talk together: What would be a respectful and safe next step?

DAY 7 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 07

🤲 Notice When Someone Needs Rest

🤲 🖼️ 💛

Everyone needs rest. Someone who feels tired may need space, quiet or adult assistance. Children should never diagnose others or monitor their health.

Try together: Choose a picture of a kind response to someone needing rest.

Talk together: What would be a respectful and safe next step?

DAY 8 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 08

🤲 Helping Without Lifting

🤲 🖼️ 💛

Lifting people or heavy objects can injure children. Instead, tell an adult when someone needs physical assistance. Imaginary picture practice is enough.

Try together: Identify which lifting picture requires an adult.

Talk together: What would be a respectful and safe next step?

DAY 9 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 09

🤲 Respect Boundaries

🤲 🖼️ 💛

Personal boundaries matter during caring. Ask before touching and stop when someone says no. Helping never requires unwanted hugs or sharing private information.

Try together: Role-play respecting a no using paper characters.

Talk together: What would be a respectful and safe next step?

DAY 10 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 10

🤲 Ask a Trusted Adult

🤲 🖼️ 💛

Trusted caregivers decide what kind of help is safe. When someone is injured, upset or needs medicine, get adult assistance rather than attempting treatment.

Try together: Practice saying 'An adult needs to help' using words or a card.

Talk together: What would be a respectful and safe next step?
