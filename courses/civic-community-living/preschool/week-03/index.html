DAY 11 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 11

🏘️ Choose a Community Idea

🏘️ 🌈 💛

We can imagine a community improvement such as a reading corner, welcoming sign or clear path. We listen to needs instead of assuming everyone wants the same thing. This is a pretend child-friendly design project, so no purchases, construction or public outreach are required.

Try together: Choose one picture idea and explain whom it could help.

Talk together: How might your idea welcome different people?

DAY 12 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 12

🏘️ Plan a Group Activity

🏘️ 🌈 💛

A good plan includes what we want to do, how we will share turns and which grown-up will supervise. Group work can include children who communicate differently or need accessible materials. We use a paper sketch and pretend roles rather than performing unsafe work.

Try together: Arrange pictures of the goal, participants, steps and a trusted caregiver.

Talk together: What is one way to include everyone?

DAY 13 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 13

🏘️ Listen to Feedback

🏘️ 🌈 💛

Feedback tells us how an idea might work better. A caregiver or classmate may notice that a sign is hard to read or a path is too narrow. We can say thank you, ask a question and consider the suggestion without teasing anyone.

Try together: Share your drawing privately and record one kind suggestion.

Talk together: What can feedback teach us?

DAY 14 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 14

🏘️ Make a Fair Revision

🏘️ 🌈 💛

Changing a plan is part of learning. We might draw larger symbols, make a path accessible or add a second activity so more people feel included. Our first idea was not a failure; it helped us notice what could improve.

Try together: Draw a before and after version of your pretend community plan.

Talk together: How did the change make your idea fairer?

DAY 15 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 15

🏘️ My Kind Community Showcase

🏘️ 🌈 💛

Show your project to a trusted caregiver using drawings, words, gestures or an appropriate device. Explain the need, the people included, the choice made and one improvement. The adult can note the learner's genuine participation separately from the knowledge quiz.

Try together: Present your picture project and explain one respectful group decision.

Talk together: What can you do to make a community welcoming?
