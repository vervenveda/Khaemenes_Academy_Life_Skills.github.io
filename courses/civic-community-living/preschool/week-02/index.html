DAY 6 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 06

🏘️ Different Voices Matter

🏘️ 🌈 💛

People have different perspectives and needs. In our civic & community living class, we can learn with a paper picture, a story, a spoken description or a safe pretend activity. Families have different abilities, languages and resources, so no purchase is needed. Ask a trusted grown-up for guidance, and never share personal information in a public project.

Try together: Listen to two pretend viewpoints.

Talk together: What did you notice, and what would you try next?
