DAY 1 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 01

🏘️ What Is a Community

🏘️ 🌈 💛

A community includes people who live, learn, work or help in a shared place. Communities can include many languages, cultures, ages and abilities. Neighbors do not all have the same resources or opinions, and everyone deserves respect. We can learn about helping without asking classmates to share their addresses or family circumstances.

Try together: Point to pictures of a library, park and neighbors; name one way people help each other.

Talk together: How can we welcome someone new?

DAY 2 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 02

🏘️ Community Places

🏘️ 🌈 💛

A library may lend books, a clinic may provide healthcare, and a park may offer a shared outdoor space. What is available varies by community. Some services may be far away or have accessibility barriers. We can use picture cards to learn about places without visiting or paying for anything.

Try together: Match pictures of a book, nurse and tree with library, clinic and park.

Talk together: What could make a community place easier for everyone to use?

DAY 3 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 03

🏘️ Kind Neighbors

🏘️ 🌈 💛

People have different routines, needs and beliefs. Kind neighbors can greet others respectfully, help when invited and understand when someone wants privacy. Children ask trusted adults before helping strangers or sharing belongings. Kindness does not require money or agreement on every issue.

Try together: Choose between two illustrated responses to a new neighbor, and explain which feels welcoming.

Talk together: How can we be kind while respecting someone's boundaries?

DAY 4 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 04

🏘️ Take Turns and Listen

🏘️ 🌈 💛

A group may want different games or stories. Taking turns gives more people a chance to participate. We can listen, ask questions and vote on a pretend classroom choice. A simple ballot lets each participant make one private choice, and we count them fairly. The winning choice does not make the other choice bad.

Try together: Use the picture-ballot activity below to vote between a storybook and a nature drawing, then count both totals.

Talk together: How do we respect someone who chose the other activity?

DAY 5 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 05

🏘️ Rules Help Us Stay Safe

🏘️ 🌈 💛

Some rules help people share spaces and stay safe, such as waiting turns or keeping pathways clear for mobility devices. Rules should be explained and applied fairly. Children can ask a trusted adult why a rule exists. In our pretend classroom, everyone may express a preference, and adults still check safety and accessibility.

Try together: Draw one fair classroom rule and explain who it helps.

Talk together: What can we do when a rule seems unfair or confusing?
