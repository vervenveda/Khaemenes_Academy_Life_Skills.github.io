## Seasonal flexibility
- **Gardening**: exchange its entire three-week block with one scheduled near planting or harvesting in the learner's **local climate**. Alternative indoor or container gardening is available for unsuitable weather or limited access.
- **Creativity / crafting**: offer an optional Christmas-themed activity for families who choose it, alongside winter, secular, cultural, and non-December alternatives. Its **full three-week course** can be moved near a locally relevant holiday.
- **Summer**: make culinary, gardening, outdoor safety, travel planning and arts available as independent standalone three-week summer modules. Summer participation does not waive essential safety checks or course mastery.
- **Starting anytime**: the calendar is illustrative; families may reorder whole courses and flex weeks without changing the 15-day completion requirement.
- **Accommodation**: age-appropriate pace, accessibility and real-world constraints determine safe activities and teaching time. Do not require unsafe seasonal work or instruction on an observed holiday.

## Standard three-week instructional rhythm
- **Week 1 / Days 1–5:** foundations, vocabulary, safety and guided investigation; formal weekly knowledge gate ≥80%.
- **Week 2 / Days 6–10:** interactive games, skill practice, applied exercises and family involvement; formal weekly knowledge gate ≥80%.
- **Week 3 / Days 11–15:** project planning, practice, capstone demonstration, final verified knowledge gate ≥80%, supervised practical approval and completed evidence packet.

No automatic course certificate from opening pages, completing games, or checking boxes. Keep learner records separate, require portfolio review, and verify score provenance before issuing completion.
