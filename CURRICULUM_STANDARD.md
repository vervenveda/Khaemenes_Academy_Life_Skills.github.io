# Khaemenes Academy — Life Skills Course Implementation Standard v1.2
**Applicability:** All 16 pathways, all five learning stages. **Status:** binding development specification; educational accreditation is not claimed.

## Program calendar and course duration (revised 2026-10-20)
- One Life Skills class spans **three instructional weeks / 15 learning days**. Each week includes five days of age-appropriate reading, play/practice, authentic activity, reflective evidence and family support.
- Week 1 foundations and safety; Week 2 practiced skills and family application; Week 3 independent or supervised capstone, final knowledge assessment, demonstration and parent/teacher review.
- Formal verified **80% knowledge mastery** is required for weekly progression. Appropriate practical safety competency is independently reviewed.
- **All 16 subjects** are included. Sixteen three-week courses require **48 instructional weeks**, fitting sequentially within **52 calendar weeks** with **four flexible weeks** for breaks, catch-up, seasonal experiences, or portfolio reviews. Concurrent enrollment is optional, not needed.
- Seasonal flexibility: offer gardening near a suitable local growing season and arts/making around locally relevant traditions or holidays (including optional December craft projects). Never assume Northern Hemisphere seasons or that all families observe the same holiday. Provide secular and culturally inclusive alternatives.
- The 16-category catalog remains intact. The 52-week plan is a sample start-anytime calendar; family-paced or summer modules may be moved while retaining three weeks / 15 instructional days per course. No forced holiday attendance or unsafe seasonal tasks.
- Existing 36-week outlines are **legacy planning drafts pending conversion**; do not represent them as already condensed, verified courses.

See [52-week seasonal calendar](./SEASONAL_52_WEEK_PLAN.md) for the proposed 13-block schedule and required double-course periods.

## Student learning day
1. Full-length visible reading/lesson with examples, objectives, vocabulary, and accessible offline version.
2. Interactive guided practice or game; randomized practice may offer hints without granting mastery.
3. Authentic daily activity (including a safe simulation where equipment, money, mobility or privacy is limiting).
4. Daily activity log with date, learner, unit, lesson, effort, evidence, reflection, and adult observation.
5. Printable Academy-branded evidence with student, date, grade/stage, course, unit and lesson.
6. Optional carefully vetted accessible instructional video with a complete original transcript/equivalent; never make online video a prerequisite.

## Mastery, progression and integrity
- Formal assessment **80% or above** is required before unlocking the next weekly unit of formal work.
- Use a reviewed bank with question variants; randomize selection and answer order per attempt; grade on the saved attempt snapshot, not mutable answer keys.
- Practice score or checking a lesson box does **not** unlock mastery.
- Scores belong to an explicitly selected learner/course/stage. Avoid attaching an anonymous preview to a student.
- Practical competencies need separate demonstrated safety approval by the parent/teacher; high knowledge scores alone cannot certify physical competence.
- Each three-week course has 15 instructional days, three weekly mastery checks, a Week 3 final practical portfolio review and a final knowledge assessment. Do not issue completion based on a first-week demonstration. Longer programs may use separate midterms where appropriate.
- Maintain attempt history and opportunity to revise after feedback. No automatic pass from page visits, time spent or log completion.
- Do not claim identity verification for anonymous local storage; describe device-local evidence as provisional until reviewed.

## Accessibility, student protection and privacy
- Offline-first single-file lessons where viable; zero mandatory external dependencies.
- Responsiveness down to 255 px, keyboard accessible controls, no sensory-only instruction, readable light-on-dark contrast, print scaling.
- Parent support prompts and opt-in private review; no public upload of student names, photos, family addresses or medical details.
- If a safety-sensitive hands-on skill cannot be supervised, use a *simulation* and mark physical verification pending.
- Video resource vetting: official source, accurate demonstrations, captions or text alternative, privacy expectations and stable embed; optional outbound watch only until vetted.
- The 80% threshold does not replace mandatory safety competency where needed.

## QA release gates
1. Reviewed factual content and substantive five-day lessons, not headings alone.
2. At least 3× formal exam length in reviewed question bank wherever practical.
3. Test wrong-answer rejection, high-score unlock, learner isolation, invalid-storage recovery, offline performance, keyboard and narrow viewport.
4. Validate printouts with Academy heading, name/date, grade/stage, unit and lesson.
5. Review suitability, accommodations, videos, and parental responsibilities by learner stage.
6. Only then replicate canonical versioned course to other campuses.

## Culinary recipe and menu ingredient policy
- The Academy's authored Culinary menus, ingredient lists, games and recipes must be **pork-free**. Do not specify pork, bacon, pork ham, lard, pork-based gelatin, pork sausage, pancetta, prosciutto or pork-derived deli meats as ingredients.
- Where an adapted traditional recipe requires a smoky, salty or savory substitute, prefer **clearly labeled turkey bacon, turkey ham or turkey sausage** where appropriate; plant-based substitutions or omitting the ingredient are equally valid. Verify product ingredient labels, since names alone do not guarantee the absence of pork-derived ingredients or allergens.
- When using turkey or other poultry, follow appropriate food-safety guidance, prevent cross-contamination, and ensure it is cooked thoroughly by a responsible adult. Preschool lessons must never direct children to cook raw poultry independently.
- Outside video links and historical references may describe other food traditions; do not imply that third-party material or all linked resources have been screened. Provide a suitable pork-free Academy alternative for any assigned recipe.
- Include a **pork-free menu QA check** before publishing each new lesson or meal-planner pack, including recipe titles, ingredient lists, shopping cards, quiz choices and imported content.
