## Mandatory reusable Preschool lesson package
1. Child-visible illustrated story or original practical reading with explicit adult safety notes.
2. Four-step accessible popup (step count, close, keyboard navigation, no dead controls) and one meaningful result visual or picture alternative.
3. Formative game (sorting, counting, coloring, sequencing) with corrective feedback and neutral, inclusive illustrations.
4. Supervised practice or accessible no-equipment simulation; no unsupervised heat, sharp tools, chemicals, choking hazards or washing machines.
5. Khaemenes Academy printable with stage, lesson number, date, name/code, drawing, practical record, caregiver notes; printable page breaks.
6. Learner-isolated storage, export, retry and a randomized teacher-reviewed knowledge bank (target >=3x quiz length). At least 80% at each knowledge gate; separate practical review and capstone before final signoff.
7. Named breadcrumb to Academy, Preschool, Life Skills, subject and week. Real links only; unavailable classes are plain labels.
8. Strict 255px and keyboard/mobile/print/offline testing before marking release browser-verified.
