Khaemenes Academy

Life Skills & Practical Independence

Free education for capable, creative, resilient lives.

This branch is the canonical Life Skills curriculum backbone across sixteen pathways. Preschool now has dedicated three-week classroom structures for all 16 pathways; Kindergarten currently has 8 dedicated courses and 8 foundation-review routes; Culinary provides the established vertical model into later grades and Higher Learning. Course maturity varies by stage, so pathway listings must be read with the release status shown for that stage.

Learning standards

Every complete course will use visible lessons, offline reading and practice, clear safety guidance, authentic demonstrations, accessible printables, and an 80% knowledge mastery gate. Demonstrated practical safety skills must be assessed separately. Midterms and finals must be independently verified before awarding credentials.

Safeguards: Young learners require adult supervision for cooking, tools, cleaning chemicals, gardening equipment and emergencies. First-aid lessons provide education, not professional certification. Entrepreneurship licensing and tax requirements must be verified for each jurisdiction. Student work stays local unless expressly exported.

Development and synchronization protocol

One source of truth: This Life Skills repository owns pathway IDs and curricular standards. Age-localized campuses will use versioned references rather than copied or automatically overwritten curricula.

Release gates: Verify lesson depth, accessibility, offline behavior, print layout, safety notes, reviewed assessments, and permissioned links before each campus sync. No automatic repository pushes or grade-placement changes.

Current status: Sixteen-pathway canonical catalog; Preschool 16/16 dedicated; Kindergarten 8 dedicated / 8 foundation-review; Culinary vertically developed through later Academy stages. Automated source regression is active, while assistive-technology speech and human print/PDF review remain explicit browser-certification gates.
