# Life Skills Cross-Pathway Parity Plan

## Purpose
Culinary Independence is the proven vertical model, but it should not become a reason to mass-produce shallow pages in the other fifteen pathways. Expansion remains gated by evidence quality.

## Current protected baseline
- Preschool: 16 dedicated pathways.
- Kindergarten: 8 dedicated pathways and 8 foundation-review routes.
- Culinary: Preschool plus Grades 1–5, Grades 6–8, Grades 9–12 and Higher Learning.
- All 16 pathways retain three-week / 15-day blueprints.

The machine-generated inventory is produced by `tools/audit_stage_parity.py`.

## Expansion order
1. Keep Preschool integrity CI green across all 16 pathways.
2. Keep automated Chromium acceptance green for Preschool and the eight dedicated Kindergarten pathways.
3. Complete the remaining eight dedicated Kindergarten pathways without converting Preschool review records into Kindergarten credit.
4. Re-run 255px, mastery, learner-switching, import/export, print and offline acceptance across all 16 Kindergarten pathways.
5. Begin one non-Culinary vertical at Grade 1.
6. Expand only after that pathway demonstrates the same lesson depth, assessment integrity, portfolio evidence and caregiver/teacher boundaries as the proven model.

## First non-Culinary candidate: Home Management
Home Management is the recommended first vertical expansion after Kindergarten reaches 16/16 because it already has mature Preschool and Kindergarten structures, naturally scales into age-appropriate household independence, and can be expanded without duplicating Culinary content.

No Grade 1 Home Management pages are authorized by this plan alone. The prerequisite is a green early-learning certification baseline and a fresh pre-audit before authoring.

## Quality rule
Parity means comparable instructional integrity—not identical page counts, identical activities, or copy-and-paste templates. Each stage must add age-appropriate reasoning, authentic evidence and independent assessment.
